Chao Tan

I am currently a second-year master's student in the Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Engineering at Tongji University, advised by Prof. Xian-sheng Hua. I am also fortunate to receive guidance from Prof. Shuxian Liang, Prof. Zhen Huang, and Prof. Chaoqun Wan.

My research interests lie in Physical AI and embodied intelligence. I aim to equip humanoid robots with human-like motor skills and cognitive capabilities for natural, reliable interaction with the physical world. Toward this goal, my work spans low-level motion control for stable, coordinated, and agile movement, alongside world models and vision-language-action (VLA) models for data-efficient policy learning and autonomous decision-making. My ultimate goal is to bridge perception, decision-making, and control, enabling robots to learn from human demonstrations and perform manipulation tasks autonomously.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing badminton and reading novels.
